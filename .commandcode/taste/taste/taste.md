# Taste
- Asks terse, direct yes/no verification questions (often phrased "does X ... or not?"), in lowercase with compact shorthand references (e.g. "tasks from 1-5, 08 and 16"), and expects concise, direct answers without preamble. Confidence: 0.7
- When reviewing task/sprint plans, focuses on cross-teammate dependencies — wants to identify which tasks are blocked waiting on other developers' work versus which are self-contained. Confidence: 0.8
- Wants task phases restructured for parallel, independent work — tasks within a phase should not depend on each other, so teammates can work concurrently without merge issues or blocking. Confidence: 0.8
- When restructuring task plans for a team, expects all teammates' work to be reorganized consistently — not just the person who prompted the revision. Will follow up to verify everyone is covered. Confidence: 0.7
- Wants explicit artifact handoff sequencing even within shared/collaborative phases — not just identifying who owns what, but the exact linear order of who produces which deliverable first so the next person can consume it and unblock themselves. Confidence: 0.75
- When the agreed plan breaks (e.g., a teammate goes rogue and pushes everything without coordination), wants pragmatic adaptation and immediate next steps — not rehashing the process or revisiting the original plan. Confidence: 0.8
